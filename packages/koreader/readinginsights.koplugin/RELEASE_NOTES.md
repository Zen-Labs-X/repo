**Changed**

- Reading streak popups
  - Tap a streak to open a **calendar** of its span: a fixed six-week month you page one at a time (arrows/swipe/keys), read days marked, and once a week has any reading its remaining days are shaded (weekly streak secured). Adjacent-month days are gray, today is bold.
  - Days, weeks and the book count show in three equal columns; the streak name sits left with the date range right-aligned.
  - Total reading time and per-day averages are now **off by default** (toggle under Settings ▸ Advanced settings ▸ Reading insight popup).

- Week start
  - The **weekly streak value**, the streak calendar's weeks, and the "most read in a week" achievement now follow the **week start day** setting (Monday/Sunday) instead of always Monday.

- Colors
  - New **Colors ▸ Reading streak calendar** settings: daily-streak day and weekly-streak gap day. Day numbers auto-pick black/white for contrast.

- Book progress calendar
  - Now a fixed six-week grid; adjacent-month days shown in gray; day cells are borderless (today stays bold).

- Popup widths
  - Streak, book progress calendar, heatmap and 8-week trend popups open at a fixed 94% width; Records is capped at 94%.
