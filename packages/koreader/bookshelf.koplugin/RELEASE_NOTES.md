**Folder styles**

- Series, authors, folders and collections can be drawn six ways: Divider card, Ribbon, Book stack, Collage, Text or None
- Set one for the whole library under Settings > Cover display > Default folder style, or give a single shelf its own by long-pressing its chip
- The picker stays open while you try styles and the shelf redraws behind it, so you can compare them
- Collage shows up to four of the group's covers; with two or three, the gap is filled with a wash drawn from the covers' own colours

**OPDS catalogues**

- Covers load automatically for the page you are on, a few at a time, rather than only for the books you tap
- They are fetched off the main loop, so paging and drilling into categories stay responsive while they arrive
- Listings are kept in a database on the device, so going back into a catalogue is instant instead of fetching it again, and a catalogue you have already opened can still be browsed with no connection (downloading and search still need one)
- The last-page chevron walks a category to its end and lands you on the last page with books on it - it counts as it goes, and a tap stops it and keeps everything already loaded
- Restarting inside a category returns you to it, on the page you left
- Each catalogue has its own settings: where its downloads are saved, and whether it refreshes on its own or only when you swipe down
