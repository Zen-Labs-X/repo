# Life Tracker for KOReader v0.1.0

A bullet journal-style life planner plugin for KOReader. Track quests, habits, mood, and reading stats directly on your e-reader.

## Features

- **Dashboard** - Morning energy check-in, daily quote, streak tracking
- **Quests** - Daily, weekly, and monthly goals with streaks and progress tracking
- **Reminders** - Time-based notifications with customizable schedules
- **Journal** - Weekly mood graph, category performance spider chart, notes
- **Reading Stats** - Integration with KOReader's reading history
- **Sleep Screen** - Optional dashboard display when device wakes

## Installation

1. Download `lifetracker.koplugin.zip` below
2. Extract to your KOReader plugins directory:
   - **Kindle**: `koreader/plugins/`
   - **Kobo**: `.adds/koreader/plugins/`
   - **PocketBook**: `applications/koreader/plugins/`
3. Restart KOReader
4. Access via **Tools > Life Tracker**
