## What’s new in v2.0

- Cleaner and more organized menu (`Care`, `Pet Info`, `Settings`, `Danger Zone`).
- New **Quick Action** option to repeat your most-used action faster.
- Improved **View Pet** screen with better spacing and cleaner information layout.
- Optional pet animation tuned for e-ink devices.
- Better status visibility in panel (clear condition badges).
- Accessories are now more meaningful with small gameplay bonuses.
- New **Today Summary** + improved **Journal** tracking daily activity and key events.
- You can now configure a gesture in KOReader to open KoPet directly (no need to go through menus every time).

## Dynamic States

- **Sick**: pet cannot eat normal food and stops XP gains until cured.
- **Bored**: appears after long periods without care and reduces XP gain.
- **Sleepy**: appears at low energy and reduces XP gain until rested.

Status badges are shown in **View Pet** (`SICK`, `BORED`, `SLEEPY`) for quick visibility.
