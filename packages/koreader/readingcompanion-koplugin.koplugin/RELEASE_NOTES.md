### Gemini Story Recaps

Story Recaps require your own Google Gemini API key.

Book text is sent to Gemini only when you explicitly choose to Generate or Update a Story Recap, and Reading Companion is designed to include text only through your current reading position.

See the README for setup and privacy details.
