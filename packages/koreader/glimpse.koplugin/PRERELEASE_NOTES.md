Pre-release for on-device testing. Since 1.2.0:

Zoom
- Double-tap default raised to 200%; new Advanced > Maximum zoom setting (150-400%).
- Optional on-screen Zoom Controls (vertical -/fit/+ strip): enable under Show Zoom Controls or Quick Actions. +/- dim at the limits; middle button returns to fit.

Menu
- New 'Enable Glimpse' master switch (disables the gesture + Open Glimpse without unbinding).
- Gesture row now reads 'Gesture to open: ...' (informational).
- New Advanced > Suppress 'format not supported' notice (off by default).
- 'Invert Images in Night Mode' moved into Advanced; captions no longer marked beta.
- Removed the separate 'Restore ignored images' entry (use the Gallery's Ignored tab).

Polish
- Disabled nav buttons keep their white background instead of going transparent.
- Zoom control zones are square and flash on press; night-mode dividers have more contrast.
- Page-dot / zoom-control alignment fixes; the dots no longer jump when zooming past fit.
