**Full Changelog**

- Added **Focus Reading** for EPUB, HTML, and XHTML books.
- Supports **Bionic Reading**, **Guided Dots**, or both together.
- Added quick Reader menu toggles:
  - `Toggle bionic reading`
  - `Toggle guided dots`
- KOReader now reloads the book automatically and tries to restore your reading position near where you left off.

## How to use

1. Put the patch file in `koreader/patches`.
2. Restart KOReader.
3. Open a supported book.
4. Open the book menu.
5. Go to **Typography**.
6. Find **Focus Reading** below **Font**.
7. Toggle **Bionic Reading**, **Guided Dots**, or both.

You can also use the Reader menu shortcuts:
- `Toggle bionic reading`
- `Toggle guided dots`
