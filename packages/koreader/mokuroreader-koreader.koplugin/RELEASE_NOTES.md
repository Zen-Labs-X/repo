## Panel Zoom Edition - v2.1.1 🔍

Everything in v2.1.0 PLUS panel zoom features!

### What's New
- **Persistent popup** - stays open during dictionary lookups
- Look up multiple words without retapping
- Works in both normal view AND panel zoom mode

### Panel Zoom Features (from v2.0.1)
- ✅ Mokuro works inside zoomed panels
- ✅ Visual tap zone indicators
- ✅ Perfect for manga with small text

### Note on Tap Zone Accuracy
Visual indicators show where mokuro detected text. Due to mokuro's detection accuracy, indicators may be slightly offset in dense pages or RTL manga. This reflects mokuro's OCR detection, not a plugin bug.

Download `mokuroreader-v2.1.1-panelzoom.zip`. Don't install both versions!

- KOReader v2025.08 or higher
